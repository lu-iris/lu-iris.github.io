Settlyfe

Role

Team
5 mobile & 4 web designers, plus frontend engineers
Timeline
Mar – Aug 2026
Tools

Settlyfe is an AI-powered rental platform serving landlords, renters, and agents. As a UI/UX design intern I designed and refined the mobile app interfaces, working from wireframes through high-fidelity UI in Figma.

My main project was an end-to-end redesign of the in-app services portal. I ran independent competitor research and restructured 10 service categories into two clear paths — instant-match and quote-based — then designed the intake forms, booking dashboards, and the foundation for a standalone native services app.

I also designed the AI-enabled document features: lease generation, digital signing, and document summarization, translating product requirements into flows that stay simple on a phone. Throughout, I presented work to the team three times a week, folded feedback in through iterative design reviews, and took part in QA and TestFlight testing.
