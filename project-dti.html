Role

Team

Cornell Digital Tech & Innovation is a student project team building software for the campus community. As a graphic designer I create the promotional graphics behind its fundraising and recruitment campaigns.

The Giving Day set was part of a multi-channel campaign that helped the team raise $8,000+. The recruitment graphics — timelines, info sessions, and event posts — supported outreach that brought in 150+ applicants, and a redesigned LinkedIn banner sharpened brand alignment alongside 175+ monthly profile views.

Each piece is designed to hold up in an Instagram feed while staying consistent with the team’s visual identity, so a viewer can recognize a DTI post before reading a word of it.
