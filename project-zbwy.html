Graphic Design

ZBWY Fashion Inc — Apparel Graphics

Role
Freelance Designer
Team
ZBWY in-house design team
Timeline
Jan – Mar 2024
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop

ZBWY Fashion, Inc. brought me on as a freelance designer to create original graphics for T-shirts and hoodies. The brief was wearable art with a point of view: pieces that carry an ocean-conservation message without turning into a slogan.

I developed two graphic families — a flower motif and an ocean motif — and adapted each across garment colors and placements. The work contributed to the sale of 500+ pieces in three months and helped expand the brand’s visibility.

I worked directly with the in-house design team, presenting ideation, revisions, and final graphics in weekly meetings.
