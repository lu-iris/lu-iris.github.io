A selection of personal and exhibition work made alongside my design practice — sculpture, mixed-media collage, and photography. It is where I test ideas about light, material, and shadow before they show up in my digital work.

As Secretary of the Art Honor Society I coordinated the design and execution of the TAS 75th Anniversary interactive installations, which engaged 2,500+ attendees, and partnered with the science, film, and music departments on a Marine Conservation Exhibition that reached 1,500+ visitors through documentaries and art installations.

The wire-and-resin and suspended-leaf pieces are lit so the cast shadow becomes half the work; the collage and photography series study the same interest in layering, from mall interiors to a lotus pond.
